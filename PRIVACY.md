# Privacy policy: Toledo Organizer

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

Toledo Organizer is a browser extension that reorganises the Toledo home page (toledo.kuleuven.be) inside your own browser. It is an unofficial project, not affiliated with KU Leuven, UCLL or Anthology (Blackboard).

## What the extension collects

**Nothing is collected or sent anywhere.** The extension has no server, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no account. The developer cannot see your data.

## What it reads and stores, locally

- **Read on the page:** while you are on `toledo.kuleuven.be`, the extension reads the course list and the "Updates en communicatie" list that Toledo already shows you (course names, course IDs, academic years, update titles and dates), so it can display them in your own structure. This stays in the page and is not stored.
- **Stored in your browser:** your own settings: groups, your course names and colours (linked to each course's Toledo code), default course versions, hidden courses, appearance preferences and your to-do list. They are kept in the extension's local browser storage on your device and are removed when you uninstall the extension or use *Reset Organizer*.
- **Export:** only when you click *Export*, your settings are saved as a JSON file to your own Downloads folder.

The extension never reads or stores your password, login cookies or grade results (for a grade notification it only sees the title shown in the updates list), and never runs on sites other than `toledo.kuleuven.be`.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Access to `https://toledo.kuleuven.be/*` | To show the organizer and dashboard on the Toledo home page. |
| `storage` | To keep your settings and tasks on your device. |
| `downloads` | To save the settings export file when you ask for it. |

## Data sharing

None. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.

## Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/SamimShafayiUCLL/toledo-organizer/issues.
